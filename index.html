Every tile is a workspace; the colored ones have windows. Click a tile to focus it. Click a window in the focused tile or any tile touching it (edge or corner; their outlines are a little brighter) to leave with that window focused. Click elsewhere in the focused tile to enter it.
Drag to pan, even from a workspace. Right-drag or the wheel rotates. Hold the move key (below) and drag a workspace to move it.
Arrows: neighbor tile. PgUp/PgDn: walk the workspaces with windows. Home: back to the center, marked with a small ring. The center follows the group: if its workspace empties, it moves to the nearest one with windows. When the group is too far to see, a yellow arrow on the edge points to it; click it to go back.
O: open a window on the focused tile. C: close one. X: close all. Enter: enter.
